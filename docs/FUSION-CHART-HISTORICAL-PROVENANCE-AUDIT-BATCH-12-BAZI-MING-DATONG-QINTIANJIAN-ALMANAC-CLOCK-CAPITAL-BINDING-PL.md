# Fusion Chart Historical Provenance Audit R1 — Batch 12PL

## Ming Qintianjian almanac clock-standard / capital binding

Status: **INSTITUTIONAL TIMEKEEPING + ALMANAC COLOCATION CLOSED / AUTOMATIC CAPITAL OR CLEPSYDRA QISHUO BINDING REJECTED / MD-G03 OPEN**

12PK closed the internal Shoushi/Datong clock label as a uniform 子正-based 100-ke coordinate, but left its physical realization and longitude unresolved. 12PL asks whether Ming institutional practice supplies the missing binding.

### 1. Qintianjian really did own the relevant official functions

(萬曆)《大明會典》卷223 places under the same 欽天監:

- 譙樓定時刻/漏刻 operations; in 正統六年 these were transferred from 順天府 to the bureau.
- 日月交食 computation with officially reported 分秒時刻; during eclipse rites the bureau's officer 專報時候.
- annual production of 《大統曆》, with the central sample sent to Nanjing and the provincial administrations for matching publication.
- 正統十一年 圭表壺漏俱如南京舊制.
- 嘉靖七年 a four-zhang gnomon was erected to 測晷以定氣朔.

This closes **institutional co-location** of official time service, astronomical event timing and almanac production.

It does **not** state that the 定朔/合朔 small remainder was read from the 譙樓 clepsydra, that the published conjunction label was local apparent/mean solar time, or that Beijing/Nanjing longitude was its zero point.

### 2. Capital location is not an automatic calendrical time standard

《明史》卷31 preserves a direct received-historical counterexample in the day/night module. After the Yongle capital move to Shuntian, Yingtian winter/summer day-night timings continued in use. Zhengtong 14 briefly changed to Shuntian values, but the Jingtai-era decision restored the Hongwu/Yongle convention rather than treating the current capital as automatically normative.

The scope is sunrise/daylength, not qishuo. Its value for 12PL is therefore negative and precise:

**CURRENT CAPITAL != AUTOMATIC REFERENCE FOR EVERY CALENDAR TIME MODULE.**

This reinforces the module-specific geography already established in 12PK; it does not select a qishuo meridian.

### 3. Observation/calibration is still not a clock-coordinate identity

The Huidian's 嘉靖七年 phrase 測晷以定氣朔 creates a genuine institutional bridge between observation and qi-shuo calibration. It does not expose the numerical mapping from a local shadow/clock observation to the published 定朔 remainder.

The already-collated 1518 Zhu Yu proposal is consistent with this separation: observatory measurement, clepsydra/day-night adjustment, Huihui comparison and Nanjing/provincial gnomon observations were proposed as multiple calibration streams; the proposal was not proved adopted as a single bureau clock standard.

### 4. Official almanac conjunction labels remain internal-coordinate controls

The six surviving official Ming almanacs with 56 conjunction-time entries remain strong production controls: D1 matches 56/56 printed bins. As 12PI established, both sides share the same unresolved historical coordinate. Institutional provenance does not turn that internal fit into Beijing/Nanjing longitude evidence.

### 5. Adjudication

Closed:
- Ming Qintianjian official clock/timekeeping service;
- official eclipse-time computation/reporting;
- central Datong almanac production/distribution;
- an institutional observation-to-qi-shuo calibration clause.

Rejected:
- same bureau -> one numeric time coordinate for every module;
- current capital -> automatic qishuo meridian;
- Qintianjian clepsydra -> published qishuo time-label identity.

Still unresolved:
- apparent solar vs mean solar vs calibrated clepsydra/conventional realization;
- Beijing vs Nanjing vs another inherited qishuo geographic reference.

Therefore MD-G03 remains **OPEN_BLOCKING_GENERAL_ADAPTER**. HPA-DAYUN-CAL-002 remains **MISSING_FROM_PRODUCT**, runtime remains fail-closed, and modern ephemeris meridian fitting remains unauthorized.
