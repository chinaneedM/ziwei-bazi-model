# Fusion Chart Historical Provenance Audit R1 — Batch 12PN

## Jingtai-1 eclipse clock provenance

Status: **CONTEMPORANEOUS OPERATIONAL DISCREPANCY CLOSED / ABSOLUTE CLOCK + PHASE + LOCALITY PROVENANCE OPEN / MD-G03 OPEN**

12PM left the qishuo absolute-time bridge unresolved. 12PN tests a stronger candidate: the Jingtai-1 lunar-eclipse timing error.

### 1. The discrepancy is already in Ming Shilu, not only in later Mingshi

《明英宗睿皇帝實錄》卷187, 景泰元年正月辛卯, records the event on the same annalistic day:

`是日早月食當在卯正三刻欽天監官以為辰初初刻致失救護六科十三道劾監正許敦等推測不明下三法司論罪當徒詔宥之`

This is materially stronger provenance than relying on Qing-compiled 《明史》 alone. It closes that an operational actual-vs-predicted timing discrepancy was recorded in the Ming court annal and immediately generated rescue failure plus censorial impeachment.

### 2. What it does not say

The target clause does not identify:

- a named observer who read 卯正三刻;
- a clepsydra, tower clock or other timekeeping instrument;
- apparent solar time, mean solar time, or any longitude;
- Beijing/順天 as the explicit coordinate of the event-time label;
- whether 卯正三刻 is 初虧, 食甚, 復圓, or another eclipse reporting convention.

The surrounding court record makes a Beijing-capital operational context highly plausible, but context is not a direct event-time locality binding.

### 3. Archive and transmission control

Academia Sinica IHP catalogs the corresponding volume-187 physical archival unit (00001d0055468), but this batch has not performed target-page image/glyph collation there. CText and Shidian provide materially agreeing digital transcriptions of the same Ming Shilu passage; they are not counted as two independent historical witnesses.

《明史》卷31 and later 《明會要》 repeat the event and remain dependent later transmissions for this purpose.

### 4. Adjudication

Closed:
- Ming Shilu-level contemporaneous official event record;
- Qintianjian prediction `辰初初刻`;
- event-time label `卯正三刻`;
- rescue failure and immediate administrative consequence.

Open:
- how `卯正三刻` was measured or reconstructed;
- which eclipse phase the bare time denotes;
- whether both labels are on a source-defined shared physical clock;
- capital/locality binding;
- apparent/mean-solar/clepsydra identity.

Therefore the Jingtai-1 record is **not yet admissible as an independent absolute-time anchor**. MD-G03 remains **OPEN_BLOCKING_GENERAL_ADAPTER**; HPA-DAYUN-CAL-002 remains **MISSING_FROM_PRODUCT**; runtime and modern meridian fitting remain fail-closed.
