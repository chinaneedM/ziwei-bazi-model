## 2. First-party publisher identity

National Library Press's first-party product page closes the 2016 facsimile set as 刘承幹《求恕斋日记（全十八册）》, ISBN 978-7-5013-5876-2, published 2016-08-11, 18 volumes, 9008 pages.

The publisher explicitly describes the edition as a full facsimile of Liu Chenggan's diary manuscripts. The surviving manuscript corpus is now divided between Shanghai Library and Fudan University Library. This batch does not assign the exact original manuscript behind volume 16 pp.321—322 to either institution without a volume-level holding map.

## 3. Volume 16 chronology from the publisher table of contents

The first-party table of contents gives the following sequence in volume 16:

- 1949 October begins p.307;
- 1949 November begins p.321;
- 1949 December begins p.335;
- 1950 January begins p.355.

Therefore the NLC article's cited pp.321—322 lie inside the **November 1949** diary section.

This is a direct page-to-month bibliographic closure. It is not a direct reading of the diary pages themselves.

## 4. Consequence for the NLC 2024 article

The article frames the episode as occurring “早在1949年底” and cites pp.321—322 after the statement that part of the Qu-family rare books were 让归政府. The publisher table of contents independently places those pages in November 1949, so the citation chronology is internally consistent.

This also requires separation from the later January-1950 Zheng Zhenduo letter controlled by reference [24]. The two events may be related historically, but they are not the same dated layer.
