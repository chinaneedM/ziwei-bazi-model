## 1. Why this batch matters

Batch 12FS established that the NLC-hosted 2024 article assigns the future priced-acquisition-intention sentence to reference [24], not [23]. Batch 12FU therefore audits the source identity and access boundary of [24] before any exact date or item-level inference is promoted.

## 2. NLC reference [24]

The NLC-hosted article cites 陈福康《郑振铎年谱》, 上海外语教育出版社, 2017, pp.1397—1398 after a January 1950 letter from Zheng Zhenduo to the Qu brothers. The article reproduces an excerpt praising the conversion of private holdings to public custody and hoping that the remaining collection could later be progressively acquired for payment under agreement.

The article itself gives only `1950年1月`. It does not expose an exact day, the complete letter, an archival file number, or the underlying publication/source note.

## 3. 2017 revised-edition identity

Shanghai International Studies University's official 2017 works list records 陈福康《郑振铎年谱（修订本）》上、中、下, 上海外语教育出版社, 2017-11-01.

A publisher-distribution storefront exposes ISBN `9787544647243`, identifies 陈福康 as author, and reproduces his 2017 new afterword. In that afterword, Chen describes the large-scale revision, thanks Shanghai Foreign Language Education Press and its editor, and dates the afterword 2017-01-01.

Therefore the cited 2017 work identity as a three-volume revised edition is closed. The project still has not directly inspected pp.1397—1398.

## 4. Tokyo Metropolitan Library holding

Tokyo Metropolitan Library's first-party Digital BookShelf lists the 2017 Shanghai Foreign Language Education Press edition as three volumes:

- 上册: `920.27 / Z11 / 10-1`;
- 中册: `920.27 / Z11 / 10-2`;
- 下册: `920.27 / Z11 / 10-3`.

This closes a current catalog holding route at shelf-listing level. It does not yet close exact material codes, current item availability, remote-copy eligibility, or which volume contains continuous pp.1397—1398.

No volume assignment is inferred from page number or volume order.
