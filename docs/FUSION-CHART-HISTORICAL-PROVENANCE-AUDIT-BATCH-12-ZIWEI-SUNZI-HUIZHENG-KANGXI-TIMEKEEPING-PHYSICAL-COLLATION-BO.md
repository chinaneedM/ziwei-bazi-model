## 4. Direct physical reading

PDF p44 physically carries the transition heading `日百刻配十二時之數天行之固` and the hundred-ke allocation discussion. Direct visual collation of the target sentence reads:

`若子時則上半時在夜半前屬昨日下半時在夜半後為今日`

The decisive mechanical semantics are therefore:

- upper half of Zi is before midnight and belongs to the previous day;
- lower half is after midnight and is the current day;
- the passage does **not** reassign the upper half of Zi to the Hai branch.

## 5. Philology / transcription control

The current Shidian public transcription renders the same sentence as:

`若子時，則上半時在夜半前，屬昨日，下半時在夜半後，屬今日`

The direct Kangxi-manuscript physical witness therefore preserves `屬昨日／為今日`, whereas the public transcription regularizes the second relation verb to `屬今日`.

This is not a mechanical divergence. It is classified as:

`DIFFERENT_WORDING_SAME_MECHANICAL_RULE`

The result is especially important because Batch 12AT directly collated the 1578 NCL 《三命通會》 physical witness with the opposite lexical asymmetry `為昨日／屬今日`, while received digital transcriptions commonly normalize both sides to `屬`. The three surfaces demonstrate that `為/屬` must be handled philologically rather than used as a false candidate split.

Strong phrase-level similarity to 《革象新書》 and 《三命通會·論時刻》 makes historical transmission/source reuse plausible, but this batch does **not** adjudicate textual genealogy or claim direct dependence.
