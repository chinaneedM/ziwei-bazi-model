## 3. Surviving 1950 manuscript range

Li Jun's published editorial preface, reproduced from the Zhonghua Book Company 1912 source article, describes the surviving 1950 manuscript as one volume titled 《养新日记》, preserving **1950-01-01 through 1950-12-06**.

Therefore 1950-01-06 is securely within the surviving manuscript range. The same preface states that fifteen diary manuscript volumes were donated by Gu Songfen to Shanghai Library.

This closes only manuscript coverage. The project has not directly reviewed the 1950-01-06 manuscript leaf.
