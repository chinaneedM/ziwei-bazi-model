## GK02538《大統曆註》：大統曆字命名作用域与“世宗期书目表”防火墙

Status: **TITLE 大統曆註 ≠ SUBTYPE Daetongryokja / KASI ITEM REMAINS 觀象監活字 / 大統曆字 NAME SPANS 大統曆 + 時憲曆 IN SECONDARY BIBLIOGRAPHIC CONTROL / GK02538 SUBTYPE AND IMPRESSION YEAR UNRESOLVED / NO RUNTIME CHANGE**

## 1. Why this batch

12EI established only a physical-scale compatibility: GK02538 nominal 21-character vertical slot ≈ 1.2048 cm versus official medium 印曆字 height 1.2 cm.

The remaining shortcut under test is: book title contains 大統曆 + catalog says 觀象監活字 => subtype 大統曆字.

12EJ rejects that shortcut.

## 2. KASI 1997 bibliographic control

Yu Gyung Ro's 1997 KASI conference paper, *Publication of Chil Jung San Nae Oe Pyun by the Great King Sejong*, gives Table 3. Item 18 is `大統曆註（觀象監活字） 跋 權近, 卞季良, 金鑌 12卷4冊`.

The paragraph introducing the table states that it contains books of Goryeo, Yuan, Ming, as well as books published in Sejong's period. Therefore table membership does not prove that every item was printed in Sejong's reign, and the target item is not relabeled 大統曆字.

## 3. Kim Sang-Ho p.132 name-scope control through an independent bibliography

A Hansung University bibliographic thesis states on PDF p.52 that `觀象監大統曆字` printed holdings include `大統曆` (1651, 1652) and `時憲曆` (1653–1723). Footnote 41 explicitly cites `金相淏, 觀象監活字考, 圖書館學 14 (1987), p.132`.

This establishes a nomenclatural firewall: 大統曆字 functions as a typographic classification and is not restricted to books whose title is literally 大統曆. Therefore the reverse inference `title 大統曆註 -> subtype 大統曆字` is not authorized.

## 4. Relationship to Kim Sang-Ho 1987

The RISS indexed abstract remains the higher-level taxonomy control, proposing Daetongryokja, Naeyongsamsoja, Myongsiryokja and Chiljongryokja and warning that generic Kwansanggam Type labels can collapse distinct types. 12EJ does not claim direct recovery of the full 1987 article.
