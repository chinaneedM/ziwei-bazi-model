## 2. Secondary scholarly control

Wang Xiaohu, “元代孙逢吉《准斋心制几漏图式》新探,” 《中国科技史杂志》 35(4), 2014, 446–458, argues that:

- the received work uses twenty-five arrows;
- its solstitial extrema are 38/62 and 62/38;
- its arrow-change dates are explicitly tied to almanac/solar-term state;
- the numerical/date system fits the Yuan Shoushi regime rather than the conventional Song 40/60 geographic-calendrical regime;
- the author is better sought among Yuan-era Yanling persons named Sun Fengji.

Tianwen classifies this article as **secondary mechanical chronology scholarship**, not a primary dating witness.

## 4. Yuan person control

A received text of Fang Hui's 《桐江集》卷一《孫君山經序》 names:

    嚴陵孫君逢吉

and describes him as studying astronomy/geography with knowledge involving the Yan Su clepsydra tradition. The passage contains a 辛巳 time marker; Wang 2014 identifies this as 1281 and proposes this Yuan Yanling Sun Fengji as the Zhunzhai author.

The geographic/subject-matter fit is meaningful, especially beside the received Zhunzhai self-label 古嚴. But the Fang Hui passage does **not** name 《準齋心製几漏圖式》. Hence:

    YANLING_SUN_FENGJI_1281 == ZHUNZHAI_AUTHOR
    POSSIBLE / NOT PROVED
