## 2. Citation sequence in the NLC-hosted article

Reference [22] follows the article's statement that, after Zheng Zhenduo's mediation, part of the Qu-family rare-book holdings were transferred to the government.

That sentence is followed by reference [24], Chen Fukang's 《郑振铎年谱》 pp.1397—1398, not [23].
