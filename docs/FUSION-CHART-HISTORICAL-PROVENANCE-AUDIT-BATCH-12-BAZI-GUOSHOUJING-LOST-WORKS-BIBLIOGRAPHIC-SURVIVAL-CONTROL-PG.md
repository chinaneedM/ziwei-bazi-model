## Guo Shoujing lost works: bibliography, survival boundary and homonym firewall

Status: **ONE-JUAN EARLY BIBLIOGRAPHIC STATE PREFERRED / SEVEN-JUAN LATE VARIANT PRESERVED / LATER HOMONYMS FIREWALLED / NO DIRECT TEXT RECOVERED / MD-G03 OPEN**

12PF identified 郭守敬《修改源流》 and 《古今交食考》 as the highest-value route for the 1294 revision log. 12PG first closes their bibliographic identity so later searches cannot silently bind the wrong text.

### Volume-count control

《元史》郭守敬傳 records 《修改源流》一卷 and 《古今交食考》一卷 and says they were 並藏之官. 《千頃堂書目》卷十三 independently repeats both one-juan counts.

《新元史》 later gives 《修改源流》七卷. Because the earlier official biography and later bibliography agree on one juan, the seven-juan reading is preserved as a late variant but is not promoted as the original work state.

### Survival boundary

並藏之官 is a Yuan custodial statement, not proof that a copy survives now. No direct public text of either Guo work has been located on the reviewed source horizon. A modern secondary source describes the works as lost, but that is not treated as exhaustive global no-survival proof.

### Homonym firewall

古今交食考 is not a unique title. The late-Ming 崇禎曆書 contains a different work of that title under 李天經 / 湯若望-related authorship, and 朱載堉's calendrical writing also uses the same title. Title-only matching is therefore forbidden.

### MD-G03 consequence

No quotation recovered in 12PG names the 1294 observation site or qishuo geographic reference. Evidence increment for the site question is zero. MD-G03 remains open and runtime stays fail-closed.
