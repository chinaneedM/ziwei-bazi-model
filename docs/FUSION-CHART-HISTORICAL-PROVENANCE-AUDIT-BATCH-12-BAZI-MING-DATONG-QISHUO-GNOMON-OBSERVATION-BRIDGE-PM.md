# Fusion Chart Historical Provenance Audit R1 — Batch 12PM

## Jiajing qishuo gnomon / observational bridge semantics

Status: **OBSERVATIONAL CALIBRATION PURPOSE ATTESTED / DIRECT GNOMON-OR-CLOCK → 定朔 NUMERIC BRIDGE NOT ATTESTED / MD-G03 OPEN**

12PL established that official timekeeping, eclipse timing and Datong almanac production were institutionally co-located under the Ming Qintianjian, but that co-location did not identify the qishuo absolute clock. 12PM tests the stronger-looking phrase **測晷以定氣朔** and related Jiajing observation programs.

### 1. 嘉靖七年：測晷以定氣朔

《明史·天文志》 records that in Jiajing 7 a four-zhang wooden gnomon was established to measure shadows and determine qi-shuo. The same passage concludes that the observatory instrument system was complete and **一以元法為斷**.

This is direct evidence for observation serving calendar determination/calibration. It is not a mechanical equation from shadow length to the numerical 定朔/合朔 small remainder. The passage does not name a qishuo locality, longitude, apparent/mean solar-time standard, or a tower-clepsydra identity.

### 2. 嘉靖二年華湘：合朔 is a verification target inside a broader observing program

The received Mingshi/Ming-Huiyao tradition preserves Hua Xiang's program: observe day and night at the observatory, keep daily/monthly records through the next winter solstice, and use the record to verify the twenty-four qi, solstices/equinoxes, **合朔**, solar/lunar positions, equatorial/ecliptic coordinates, middle stars and planetary phenomena.

This proves that 合朔 was meant to be checked against an observational program. It still does not specify how one local shadow/clock reading is converted into the qishuo fractional-day coordinate.

Received sources differ on the proposal's immediate administrative disposition. 12PM therefore uses the proposal only for intended verification semantics and does not claim a completed Jiajing-2 production dataset.

### 3. 1518 direct physical control: calibration streams were plural

The directly collated Wuzong Shilu Zhu Yu proposal already separates observatory measurement, Clepsydra Office day/night calibration, Huihui-calendar verification, and Nanjing/Zhejiang gnomon observations. The proposal was not proved adopted.

This is important because it blocks a false collapse:
**GNOMON + CLEPSYDRA + CALENDAR VERIFICATION != ONE AUTOMATIC QISHUO CLOCK COORDINATE.**

### 4. Philological boundary of 「以定氣朔」

For this project, 「以定氣朔」 may safely support the institutional/calibrational proposition that shadow observations contributed to determining or correcting the calendar's qi-shuo system.

It may not be expanded into:
- the gnomon directly measures lunar conjunction;
- qishuo is Beijing apparent solar time;
- qishuo is Beijing mean solar time;
- qishuo is the Qintianjian tower-clepsydra reading;
- observatory location automatically selects the qishuo longitude.

No reviewed source supplies that missing operator.

### 5. Adjudication

Closed:
- Jiajing-era observational calibration/verification of calendar outputs is real;
- 合朔 is explicitly among the verification targets in the Hua Xiang program;
- Jiajing-7 gnomon construction is explicitly tied to 定氣朔 in received official history.

Not closed:
- local gnomon/clock -> numerical 定朔 remainder;
- local observed time -> published qishuo clock label;
- Beijing/Nanjing/apparent/mean/clepsydra realization.

Therefore MD-G03 remains **OPEN_BLOCKING_GENERAL_ADAPTER**; HPA-DAYUN-CAL-002 remains **MISSING_FROM_PRODUCT**; runtime and modern ephemeris longitude fitting remain fail-closed.
