## 1. Scope

Batch 12AP established that the 1581 Jielan publication line contains an inclement-weather birth-time discussion at PT49, while Batch 12AJ/AK kept the distinct Fullbook `羅經以定真確時候` operational question open.

Batch 12BQ asks whether the Jielan phrase family is already attested substantially earlier. The answer is yes at the textual level, but the evidence must be dated in layers rather than collapsed into a single “1408 physical witness”.

## 2. Exact locus in 《永樂大典》卷18764

Public volume transcription places the passage under:

`命〈諸家星命百二十六〉 → 前定數 → 諸家序 → 四字經序`.

The convergent public transcription reads, without modern punctuation:

`且子丑寅亥四箇時辰難以推分古經云天陰雨露時難定便是神仙也有差`

This joins two points in one local context: (1) 子、丑、寅、亥 are difficult to distinguish precisely; and (2) an explicitly quoted `古經` says inclement weather makes the time difficult to determine, even for an expert.

The Wikisource page layer binds this material to `永樂大典18764.pdf/3`. The Commons file page describes a 42-page, 7.01 MB scan. CText and Shidian independently expose the same textual sequence as public transcription/navigation controls.

## 4. Dating firewall: Yongle compilation versus Jiajing physical copy

National Library of China material states that 《永樂大典》 was compiled in the Yongle reign, that only one original was first produced, that another copy was transcribed in the Jiajing reign, and that the Yongle original is lost while extant copies are Jiajing duplicates.

Accordingly this batch records two different dates/scopes:

- **textual incorporation layer:** the passage is present in the Yongle Dadian compilation tradition, giving an incorporation terminus ante quem no later than completion of the original compilation (1408 conventionally used for the completed Yongle project);
- **extant physical-recension layer:** the surviving manuscript tradition is the Jiajing duplicate, not an extant 1408 original leaf.

NLC control: `https://www.nlc.cn/pcab/zx/xw/20260702_2653756.shtml`.

The internal phrase `古經云` indicates the compiler/source text itself claims an earlier textual antecedent, but the identity and date of that `古經` are unresolved. It is not permissible to turn that formula into a dated pre-Yongle source without locating the cited work.

## 5. Relation to 1581 《紫微斗數捷覽》

Existing Batch 12AP evidence binds Jielan PT49 / 《論十二生時難定訣》 to the index fragment `天陰雨下時難定`. Current public transcription continues `便是神仙也有差` and places the discussion around 亥、子、丑 birth-time ambiguity.

The comparison is therefore:

```text
Yongle-Dadian 四字經序: 天陰雨露時難定，便是神仙也有差
1581 Jielan:             天陰雨下時難定，便是神仙也有差
```

This is strong phrase-level and semantic continuity. It establishes an **early shared inclement-birth-time formula/motif**, but not direct copying, author identity, or a single school lineage. `雨露` versus `雨下` is preserved as a textual variant rather than silently normalized.
