## 1. Scope

Batches 12BH–12BO closed the current Japanese-copy and homologous-timekeeping evidence boundaries around the Shidian token `上四亥`. The exact Jiajing-44 National Archives volume-three target leaf remains unread.

Batch 12BP asks a different provenance question: did an independently documented Chinese historical holding of the same title exist, and can that route presently yield a physical target leaf?

## 2. Jiaqing Tianyige shelf-inventory evidence

The received 《天一閣書目》 lists `筮箧理数日钞二十卷` among the Tianyige holdings.

The catalog preface is operationally important: Ruan Yuan states that in Jiaqing 8–9 he ordered Fan-family attendants to enter the pavilion and copy the holdings by book cabinet (`登閣分廚寫編`). This supports classifying the title entry as a historical holding/shelf-inventory record rather than a generic bibliography copied from elsewhere.

Evidence URL: `https://www.shidianguji.com/book/NA05256/chapter/1kyb3net1ge1v`.

## 3. Guangxu-era continued holding record

The later 《天一閣見存書目》 still records:

`筮箧理数日钞二十卷，明柯佩编。`

The National Library of China catalog-history page independently identifies Ruan Yuan's 《天一閣書目》 and Xue Fucheng's 《天一閣見存書目》 as representative Tianyige collection catalogs, and notes the 1995 《新編天一閣書目》 as the first openly published post-1949 catalog.

Evidence URLs: `https://www.shidianguji.com/book/NGJ892002318084315185/chapter/1lul9utbqbn6u` and `https://www.nlc.cn/pcab/ztzl/gjpcml/page5/201810/t20181026_173280.htm`.

This establishes continuity of the **historical cataloged title** into the late Qing. It does not by itself establish present-day survival.

## 4. Zhejiang collection-catalog corroboration

《浙江採集遺書總錄》 records the title as `筮箧理数日抄二十卷刋本`, attributes it to `明柯佩輯`, and gives a brief content profile beginning with book-formula/Qimen and Later-Heaven Hongfan Eight-Trigram/Five-Phase material.

Evidence URL: `https://ctext.org/wiki.pl?chapter=317234&if=en&remap=gb`.

This is useful bibliographic corroboration. It is not a target-leaf glyph witness.

## 5. Attribution and edition boundary

The National Archives/Shidian physical-copy transcription layer identifies the Japanese set with `一壺天俱道人校編` and related title-page/editorial wording. The Tianyige/collection catalogs instead attribute the work to `明柯佩編/輯`.

Batch 12BP does **not** identify these names as the same person, pseudonyms, or equivalent responsibility statements. It records an attribution tension requiring separate bibliography/philology.

Likewise, matching title and twenty-juan extent do not prove that the Tianyige historical holding is the same impression, same textual recension, or same physical copy as the 1565 National Archives set.
