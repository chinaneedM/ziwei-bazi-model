## Ming Datong qishuo geographic identifiability audit

Status: **56-BIN INTERNAL REPLAY QUANTIFIED / ABSOLUTE MERIDIAN NOT IDENTIFIABLE FROM INTERNAL PAIRS / MD-G03 OPEN / NO RUNTIME SELECTION**

The existing 56 conjunction-time controls are exceptionally strong for validating D1 against surviving official Ming almanac clock labels. They are not, by themselves, an absolute geographic reference experiment.

Using the modern Beijing–Nanjing longitude delta from the existing Mihn et al. control (about 9.8667 minutes of local solar time), the research-only diagnostic gives:

- D1 without coordinate shift: 56/56 inside printed bins;
- D1 shifted east by the Beijing→Nanjing delta: 22/56;
- D1 shifted west by the same delta: 17/56;
- sexagenary-day crossings under either shift: 0;
- nearest event to a 子正 day boundary: 1616 month 4, about 17.28 minutes away, still farther than the Beijing–Nanjing offset.

These counts do NOT establish the no-shift coordinate as Beijing, Nanjing, or any other place. The printed clock bins and D1 reconstructions are two descriptions in the same hidden historical coordinate. Shifting only D1 changes frames; it does not compare either side with an independent absolute-time observation.

Therefore the strong 56/56 fit closes algorithmic/internal-coordinate consistency, while the absolute geographic zero point remains unidentifiable from this corpus alone.
