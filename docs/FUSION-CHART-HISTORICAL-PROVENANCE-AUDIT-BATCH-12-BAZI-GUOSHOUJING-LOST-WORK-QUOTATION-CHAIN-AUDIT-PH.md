# Fusion Chart Historical Provenance Audit R1 — Batch 12PH

## Guo Shoujing lost-work quotation-chain audit

Status: **LATER REVISION NARRATIVE CONVERGENCE ATTESTED / DIRECT LOST-WORK QUOTATION NOT RECOVERED / SITE WORDING NOT RECOVERED / MD-G03 OPEN**

12PG controlled the bibliographic identity of 郭守敬《修改源流》 and 《古今交食考》. 12PH asks the stricter question: can the 1294 three-response revision narrative be explicitly traced to either lost work by a source marker such as 「《修改源流》曰」 or 「《古今交食考》云」?

### Result

Repeated source-marker searches across Wikisource, CText, Shidian and indexed public text surfaces recovered no such attribution.

邢雲路《古今律曆考》 clearly narrates that Guo revised the response constants again in 至元三十一年, but it does not identify 修改源流 or 郭氏古今交食考 as its source.

《明史稿》 and 《明史》 preserve the sharper narrative that the fifth-month full-moon eclipse differed by two ke and triggered adjustment of the response constants, but the reviewed passages likewise do not explicitly cite either lost work and do not name an observation site.

### Firewall

Therefore:

- later narrative convergence = real transmission evidence;
- later narrative convergence != recovered lost-work quotation;
- same content != proof that the compiler copied 修改源流 or 郭氏古今交食考;
- no place-name may be retrofitted into the lost work from later institutional context.

The lost-work quotation route is parked unless a materially new witness appears. MD-G03 remains open and the historical calendar adapter remains fail-closed.
