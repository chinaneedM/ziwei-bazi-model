## 1. Why this calibration matters

A formal 2010 Palace Museum article provides an exact page citation to `《冀淑英文集》pp.383—385`. Because the current research is trying to recover 冀淑英's Tieqin narrative and the exact page range of `《百川归海，蔚为大观》`, those numbers could easily be mistaken for a direct page locator.

Direct review of the citing article's printed pp.62–63 shows that this inference is not authorized.
