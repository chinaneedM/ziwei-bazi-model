## 2. Deng Rui's first-person editorial control

A Nanjing University page authored by Deng Rui states that while she was preparing 《五石斋文史札记》 for continuous publication in 《中国典籍与文化》 from 2001 onward:

> 每次清样，笔者均查对原书。

In the surrounding text, the material being organized is her father's 《五石斋日记》. This directly closes an **editor-checked serial transcription route**: the serial proofs were not merely copied from an unidentified later retelling; Deng Rui says she checked each proof against the original source.

This does **not** make the serial a facsimile, does not establish that every diary entry was included, and does not close the exact serial issue/page for 1950-01-29.
